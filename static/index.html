    How it works
    Hold SOXL by default. When a 1h bar closes with ADMF red on SOXS or on SOXL, go to cash for the next 4 bars (a new red restarts the count). If SOXL turns aqua or SOXS turns orange during that time, buy back early. The call uses the last finished 1h bar; act on it at the next bar's open. Uses the ADMF defaults (length 14, lookback 20): other settings test noticeably worse.
